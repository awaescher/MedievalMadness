# Release notes

Short, player-facing text of the GitHub releases (details and technical notes: `CHANGELOG.md`).
The CI takes the section `## Unreleased` (only what is new since the previous release; while it is empty the section
`## <major>.<minor>.0` of a new minor version). One line per change, written for players.

## Unreleased

## 1.11.0

- **Rewards you can see**: a weapon you earn (supply crates, wrecked buildings, felled trees, fires ...) floats up from where it happened, with its icon, the count and the player's name in their colour.
- **Chaos mode with fewer cows**: your own cow dying pays 2 cows, any other animal of your camp 1, nothing else. The "own goal" powder keg now needs a whole building, not just a palisade.
- **Auto-place option**: catapults and palisades can be placed automatically (match option, host only online).
- **Everything follows the ground**: rocks, bushes, flowers, animals and settlers lean with slopes and sink with craters and landslides; no more flames, puddles or settlers floating in the air.
- **Player names in their colour** in the feed, toasts and banners.
- **Cleaner HUD**: new catapult icons, an announcer banner that fits the window, scrollable menu options and a smaller aim box.
- **Fixes**: the loading screen is translated, burning settlers and animals no longer leave their flame behind, puddles never lie on slopes.
- **Downloads**: one ZIP per platform (Windows, macOS, Linux); the macOS app is ad-hoc signed.
